One page of the world's news for Monday 23 November 2026: a newspaper front page summing up the day in a single image. The masthead names the date, and thirty verified stories from across the globe fill the page — the COP31 climate talks collapsing without a deal in Antalya, a Russian drone hitting a block of flats near Kyiv, the International Court of Justice ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, a Russian cargo ship still burning in the Gulf of Finland, a tanker ablaze in the Strait of Hormuz, a factory fire in Indonesia, Nepal's prime minister in Delhi, India's record 720/9 in Wellington, and a UTC timeline of the day — plus a procedural world map marking where each story happened and three drawn picture panels (COP31, Kyiv, Hormuz). Every story was checked against live reporting (Reuters, AP, CNN, UKMTO, Axios, BBC) on 23 November 2026. The image is drawn entirely in code by `main.py` (Pillow only, no network, deterministic).

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

This writes `out/entry.png` (1024×1024, 49,771 bytes).
